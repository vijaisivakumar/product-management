# Product & Technology Department — Business Review

## About This Department

The Product & Technology team owns the Swiggy app and platform experience across
customer, restaurant-partner, and rider surfaces. This team drives feature development,
app performance and reliability, user-experience design, experimentation (A/B testing),
and the product roadmap. Every tap, search, and checkout on the platform is shaped
by the decisions this team makes.
