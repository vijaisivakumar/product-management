# Logistics & Delivery Department — Business Review

## About This Department

The Logistics & Delivery team runs Swiggy's last-mile engine — the delivery rider
fleet that carries every order from the restaurant's kitchen to the customer's door.
This team manages rider onboarding and retention, order allocation and routing,
delivery-time performance, fleet utilisation, and the cost economics of each drop.
When food arrives hot and on time, it is this team's operations working behind the scenes.
